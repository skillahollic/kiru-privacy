Kiru — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Kiru channel's publishing software collects, how it uses that information, how long it keeps it, and how you can ask us to delete it or revoke our access.

"Kiru", "we", "us" and "our" mean the Kiru channel and its owner, who operates the software described here.

1. What Kiru is

Kiru is a channel of short videos about manifestation, published on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. Kiru is an AI-generated character: the AI double of a real person who has given signed consent to the use of their likeness and voice. Every video description says the content is AI-generated.

The videos are produced by private software that runs on a single computer owned by the channel's owner. It is not a public app. Nobody else signs in to it, and it has no user accounts other than the owner's.

2. Summary

  • The software connects only to the channel's own YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts, using the owner's permission.
  • It reads public YouTube and Reddit content to research video topics.
  • It does not collect viewers' names, email addresses, contact details or other personal information, apart from what appears in public comments and posts it reads for research (see section 4).
  • It receives viewer statistics only as aggregate numbers (for example, total views or average watch time), never as data about an identified person.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade any data. We do not run advertising or tracking of our own.
  • This website (the page you are reading) sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

3. YouTube API Services

Kiru uses the YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API).

By using Kiru's YouTube features, or by watching or interacting with Kiru's content on YouTube, you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms

Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy: http://www.google.com/policies/privacy

3.1 Data from the channel's own YouTube account (with the owner's permission)

With the owner's permission, granted through Google's sign-in (OAuth), the software:

  • uploads Kiru's videos to the channel, with their title, description, tags and the "altered or synthetic content" (AI) disclosure setting;
  • reads back each uploaded video's status and settings, to confirm the AI disclosure was applied and whether the video is public;
  • changes a video's visibility (for example, to private) when needed;
  • reads the channel's own analytics, such as views, watch time, audience retention and engagement, as aggregate numbers.

We use this data only to publish Kiru's videos and to understand which videos perform well, so we can make better ones. It is never used to identify or contact individual viewers.

3.2 Public YouTube data (for topic research)

The software uses the YouTube Data API to search public YouTube videos about manifestation and read their public information: titles, descriptions, view and like counts, publication dates, and a limited number of public comments.

Comments are read for their text only, to understand the questions viewers ask about a topic. We do not use commenter names or channel information to identify, profile or contact anyone.

4. Meta (Instagram and Facebook)

Kiru uses Meta's Instagram Graph API and Facebook Graph API, through a Meta app connected only to the channel's own Instagram account and Facebook Page, with the owner's permission (Facebook Login).

With that permission, the software:

  • publishes Kiru's videos (Reels) to the channel's own Instagram account and Facebook Page, with captions and Meta's AI-generated label;
  • reads back each post's status and label, to confirm it published correctly;
  • deletes the channel's own posts when needed;
  • reads the account's own insights, such as reach, plays and engagement, as aggregate numbers.

The software does not read the personal profiles, friends, followers' details or messages of any Instagram or Facebook user.

Meta's handling of your information is described in the Meta Privacy Policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy

5. Reddit

Kiru uses the Reddit Data API to read public posts in manifestation-related communities (subreddits), to research which topics and questions people are discussing. It reads post titles, text and scores.

It does not post, comment, vote or send messages on Reddit, and it does not use Reddit usernames to identify, profile or contact anyone.

Reddit's handling of your information is described in the Reddit Privacy Policy: https://www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy

6. Other services that process data

To rank research topics and write scripts, the software sends public text (video titles, descriptions, public comments and public Reddit posts) to Claude, an AI service from Anthropic. It does not send account credentials, private data, or commenters' names or account details. Anthropic's handling of data is described in its privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

No data obtained through the YouTube API Services, Meta or Reddit is shared with anyone else.

7. How data is stored and protected

  • All data is stored locally on the owner's own computer, in a private database and private folders. It is not stored on any public server or cloud service, apart from the platforms themselves.
  • The software and its dashboard are reachable only by the owner, over a private encrypted network.
  • Access tokens for YouTube, Meta and Reddit are kept in the computer's protected credential store (Windows Credential Manager), not in files or code.
  • Backups of the database are kept on the same private storage.

8. How long data is kept

  • The channel's own data (its videos' details, publishing records and aggregate analytics): kept while the channel operates, because it is the channel's own record.
  • Public research data from YouTube and Reddit (titles, statistics, comment and post text): refreshed or deleted within 30 days of being retrieved; backup copies that contain it are removed within a further 30 days. We may keep our own notes derived from it, such as a topic idea and a link to a source video, which contain no personal information.
  • Access tokens: kept until the owner disconnects the account or you revoke access (see section 9), then deleted.

9. How to revoke access and request deletion

Google / YouTube: you can revoke Kiru's access to a Google account at any time through Google's security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions

Meta (Instagram and Facebook): you can remove Kiru's app from a Facebook or Instagram account in the "Apps and websites" section of that account's settings (for Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=applications).

Reddit: Kiru reads only public Reddit content and is not connected to anyone's personal Reddit account, so there is no access to revoke. Reddit users can review apps authorized on their own account at https://www.reddit.com/prefs/apps.

Deletion requests: if you believe Kiru holds any information about you, for example the text of a public comment or post you wrote, email us at the address below and tell us what it is. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. Once access is revoked, the software stops retrieving data from that account, and data obtained through it is deleted within 30 days, except the channel's own published-video records.

10. Children

Kiru's content is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how Kiru collects or uses data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
